## Overview

**Presidium: A Nation in Your Hands** is a presidential governance simulation engineered in Python and Tkinter for an AI lab course.

The system places the player at the helm of national executive decision-making, evaluating the outcomes of policy directives across economic, diplomatic, and public welfare sectors.

## Core Features & AI Architecture

- **Supervised Learning Component**: Employs classification models to analyze player choice trajectories and predict socio-economic stability indices.
- **Dynamic Decision Grading Engine**: Scores policy decisions against multi-dimensional constraint matrices, balancing fiscal deficits against public satisfaction.
- **Desktop Graphical Interface**: Custom desktop simulation built with Python and Tkinter delivering responsive interactive dashboards.

## What I Learned

Translating multi-attribute utility theory and game-theoretic policy graphs into programmatic Python logic while integrating supervised classification to evaluate user decisions.
