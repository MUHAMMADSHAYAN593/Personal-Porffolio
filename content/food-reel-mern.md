## Overview

A modern full-stack platform combining video-centric social discovery with culinary storefront management.

GitHub Repository: [MUHAMMADSHAYAN593/Reels-App](https://github.com/MUHAMMADSHAYAN593/Reels-App)

## Features

- **Partner Hub**: Profile onboarding, business details, and short video reel uploading via Multer.
- **Consumer Discovery**: Responsive feed for discovering dishes and viewing preparation videos.
- **Modular Backend**: Clean separation between controllers, services, and route boundaries.
- **Security**: JWT authentication persisted via secure HTTP-only cookies.
