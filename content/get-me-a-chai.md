## Overview

**Get Me A Chai** is a creator support platform built on Next.js 15 and styled with Tailwind CSS.

Live Application: [Get Me A Chai on Vercel](https://get-me-a-chai-amber.vercel.app/)

## Technical Exploration

- **Next.js 15 App Router**: Practiced server and client component boundaries with responsive layouts.
- **GitHub OAuth**: Seamless authentication with NextAuth session management.
- **Creator Dashboard**: Real-time contribution logs and public profile customization.
