## Overview

**Roomify** is a 3D architecture design builder engineered to help users imagine, shape, and visualize architectural spaces in an intuitive, modern browser environment.

Live deployment: [Roomify on Vercel](https://roomify-three-indol.vercel.app/)

## What I Built & Redesigned

This release focused on elevating the product identity to reflect a professional architectural studio in the browser:
- **Clean Architectural Palette**: Introduced a refreshed light theme with restrained, architectural neutral tones.
- **Technical Typography**: Adopted sharp, modern monospace-accented typography for dimensions and tool controls.
- **Product-Focused Hero**: Re-architected the landing hero to immediately demonstrate spatial planning workflows.
- **Custom 3D Visual Element**: Added custom 3D representations communicating spatial depth and room structuring.
- **Centralized Styling System**: Tokenized all colors, dimensions, and borders to ensure consistent visual language across builder tools and modals.

## What I Learned

Designing creative spatial tools requires extreme visual restraint: the interface should remain subtle and unobtrusive so that the user's architectural creation remains the focus.
